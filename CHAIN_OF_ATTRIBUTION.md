## How to add yourself

The Chain of Attribution is an honor, freely taken and freely given. You do not need permission to add your name. You do not need to ask the previous Publishers. You only need to have done something with the Work.

By the terms of this Standard, if you exercise *any* of the rights granted by the License — even the absolute minimum — you hold the honor of adding your name to this Chain. 

You may add your name if you have done **any** of the following:

- **Redistribution:** You republished or shared the Work, or a Version, in its original form without any modifications.
- **Minor Modification:** You compressed, formatted, translated, fixed a bug, or made any change that does not alter the core function or meaning.
- **Substantial Modification:** You remixed, edited, changed the voice, altered the text, added new features, or created a derivative work.
- **Combination:** You included the Work, or a Version, inside a larger project, compilation, or collection.
- **Commercial Use:** You sold, licensed, or used the Work in a commercial product or service.
- **Hosting:** You mirrored, hosted, or made the Work available on a different platform or server.

If your action allows another person to see, use, or receive the Work or your Version of it, you have the right to add your name.

### How to write your entry

1. Copy the format of the entry above.
2. Place your entry at the **very top** of the list.
3. Remove the **(Me)** marker from the previous entry, and add it to yours.
4. Under your name, write the new `Work` name and `URL` where your version is hosted. If it is the exact same work with no changes, write the same Work name and your new URL. If you modified it, write your new title.
